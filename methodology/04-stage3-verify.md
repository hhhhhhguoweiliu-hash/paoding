# Stage 3 — Evidence Verification

## Five evidence gates
1. **Traceability** — can the claim point to concrete evidence IDs?
2. **Level correctness** — is observation separated from inference?
3. **Alternative check** — could another workflow explain the same evidence?
4. **Reproduction relevance** — would this decision materially affect output similarity?
5. **Falsifiability** — is there a practical test that could weaken or reject the claim?

Claims that fail traceability cannot graduate above `U/H`. Claims with no useful reproduction impact may stay in the audit but should not bloat the minimal recipe.
