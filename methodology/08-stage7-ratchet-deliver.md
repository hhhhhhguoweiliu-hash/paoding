# Stage 7 — Ratchet & Deliver

## Goal
Make failures useful and preserve auditability.

## Deliverables
- final recipe / production skill;
- reproduction records;
- comparison summary;
- unresolved hypotheses;
- audit trail;
- next action.

## Ratchet rule
Any failed or surprising reproduction becomes a regression or boundary test. Update version history rather than silently rewriting the previous result.
