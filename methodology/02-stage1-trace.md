# Stage 1 — Trace

## Goal
Turn the finished artifact into timestamped, auditable evidence.

## Video evidence
- Container, codec, dimensions, fps, duration, audio stream metadata.
- Fixed-interval evidence frames; later versions may add scene-cut and salience sampling.
- Visible text, composition, subject, camera framing, motion, transition and generation artifacts.
- Optional audio extraction for rhythm, voice, music and SFX analysis.

## Evidence rule
Every entry gets an evidence ID and `O` level unless it is explicitly unknown. Interpretation belongs in Stage 2.
